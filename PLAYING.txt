Cosmo Enhanced
==============

An independently maintained fork of the Cosmo port, focused on playing
Cosmo's Cosmic Adventure on Windows and Android.

Download and play
-----------------

Windows x64
  Extract the entire ZIP and open cosmo.exe. Select Forbidden Planet to play
  Episode 1. If you own Episode 2 or 3, select it in the launcher and point
  the file picker to the matching COSMO2.STN or COSMO3.STN. Keep the matching
  VOL file in the same folder as the STN file.

Android ARM64 and x86-64
  Install the APK and select Forbidden Planet. Touch controls are enabled
  by default. Keyboards and gamepads are also supported.
  To import Episode 2 or 3, select it in the launcher and tap Choose files.
  Touch and hold the matching STN file, tap its VOL partner, then tap Select.

Both downloads include the shareware Episode 1, Forbidden Planet.

Controls
--------

Keyboard
  Arrow keys move, Space jumps, and Alt drops a bomb. F1 opens the in-game
  help menu. On Windows, F11 or Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen.

Gamepad
  Use the D-pad or left stick to move and A to jump. Open Game Settings
  and choose Gamepad Controls for the full layout.

Android touch
  Use the directional arrows, jump and bomb buttons in the HUD. Tap Pause
  to open the in-game menu and use Select and Back to navigate. The Android
  system Back button acts as Escape. Use the device keyboard to enter your
  name on the high-score screen.

Open Game Settings to remap keyboard controls and adjust music volume,
sound effects volume, or the display filter. Use the game's Quit option
to save your high scores.

Customise
---------

Choose Customise in the episode launcher to change the soundtrack, sound
effects, display filter, character colours, or gamepad rumble. Smooth Gameplay
smooths scrolling, character movement, and animations. It starts enabled on
fresh Android installs.

On Windows, turn on Widescreen to see more of the level. Android uses
widescreen gameplay. To hide the touch HUD on Android, turn off Touch Controls
in Customise.
